About AW Musik

Founded in 2018, AW Musik is an exclusive independent record label and parent music distribution network. Evolving beyond electronic and instrumental roots into Hip-Hop and multi-genre soundscapes, we power global metadata management, publishing, and supply-chain infrastructure for partnered sub-labels and diverse indie rosters spanning Pop, Folk, Hip-Hop, and Regional Indian music worldwide.
